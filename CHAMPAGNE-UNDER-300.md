## Sub-750ml champagnes under 300 kr

**26 wines** — mostly 375ml half-bottles and 200ml piccolos. Prices from 159 kr (Nicolas Brobergière 375ml) to 299 kr.

## Why 9 of 11 are invisible

The scoring pipeline requires 25+ Vivino reviews OR a Wine Enthusiast expert score. These 9 champagnes have neither — they are lower-profile producers (Comtesse de Neples, Louis Massing, Philippe De Nantheuil, Nicolas Brobergière) that Vivino's crowd hasn't reviewed enough.

This is a **data gap**, not a quality gap. A 259 kr champagne that nobody has reviewed may still be good — we simply cannot rank it.
