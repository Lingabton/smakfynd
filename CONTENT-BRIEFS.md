# Content Briefs — Tier A Pages (Crawled-Not-Indexed)

These 15 pages have been seen by Google and rejected. Structural fixes (formats, badges, links, determinism) are deploying. This brief addresses what remains: **the content itself.**

Common pattern across all 15: ~400-600 words of generated prose, no original analysis, no author voice, no information unavailable elsewhere. Every intro could have been written by any wine site's CMS template.

---

## Priority order (effort-to-improvement ratio)

### 1. `/vin-till-pizza/` — Position 4.9, 13 impressions, 3 clicks
**Why first:** Already on page 1. Tiny content improvement could push into top 3.
**What's thin:** Generic intro about "Italian wines go well with pizza." No distinction between margherita, quattro formaggi, bianca, or Neapolitan vs American-style.
**Competitors have:** Vivino's "Wine with Pizza" article: 2,000+ words, specific wine-by-pizza-type pairings, images, user ratings per pairing.
**Gabriel's angle:** The Grythyttan perspective — pizza is arguably the most temperature-sensitive pairing in casual dining. A cold Sangiovese kills the tomato pairing. The body of the pizza dough (thin Neapolitan vs thick American) changes whether you want acidity or tannin. Nobody in Swedish wine writing covers this because nobody teaches food pairing with Neapolitan pizza at Grythyttan.
**Effort:** 30 min. One decision table (pizza type × wine style) is the entire differentiator.

### 2. `/vin-till-lax/` — Position 43.7, 35 impressions, 0 clicks
**Why second:** 35 impressions but zero clicks = snippet is not compelling. Position 43 is page 5 — Google shows it but far back.
**What's thin:** "Lax och vin — en klassisk kombination." No distinction between gravad, rimmad, varmrökt, ugns, sushi-grade rå.
**Competitors have:** Systembolaget's own guide: 1,500 words, preparation-specific pairings. Vinbetyget: critic picks.
**Gabriel's angle:** Gravad lax med hovmästarsås is Sweden's most common lax preparation, and the dill+mustard+sweetness profile is actually one of the hardest wine pairings. An honest "this is difficult and here's why" opening is more credible than "vitt vin passar bra."
**Effort:** 45 min. Needs a preparation × wine matrix.

## What every page needs (structural, not editorial)

1. **One claim the data supports that competitors cannot make.** The price history, the cross-category comparison, the scoring gap between crowd and expert — these are exclusive assets.
2. **Specificity that proves the writer has drunk wine, not just ranked it.** Temperature, glassware, decanting time, food preparation method — these are Grythyttan details.
3. **An honest limitation.** "Vi har bara kunnat betygsätta 4 av 11 chilenare under 100 kr" is more trustworthy than silence, and it's the kind of disclosure that E-E-A-T rewards.
